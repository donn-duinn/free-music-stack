# 🎸 THE ANTI-BIG-TECH MUSIC STACK — AI here to PAY artists, not replace them

## THE THESIS
Artists fear AI — and they're right to fear the *extractive* kind: models trained on their work without
consent, slop flooding the platforms, creative jobs vanishing. **Big tech points AI at artists to take from them.**

We point AI at big tech to **give**. Every tool below was built fast, by one self-taught musician working with
AI, and every single one routes **100% of the money to the artist or venue** and takes **no cut**. Same
technology, opposite direction. This is the proof: *AI isn't coming for artists — here it's here to pay them.*

## WHY IT'S THE GRANT PROJECT (Music Works Project stream, $10–40k)
Music Works funds *"innovative products, services and technologies"* that deliver *"broader artistic,
business and sector outcomes… industry development."* This is exactly that — not one tool, but **arts-sector
infrastructure that takes live music back from extractive platforms and keeps the money with Victorian
artists and venues.** The grant funds building out the stack, hardening it, documenting it, one-command
deploy, pilot venues, and onboarding — so it becomes the local default.
