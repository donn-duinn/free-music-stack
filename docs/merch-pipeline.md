# 🎽 openmic MERCH PIPELINE — get merch to the show

**The insight:** open-mic slots booked *in advance* = a fulfilment window. Enough lead time to get an
artist's merch to the gig so they can actually sell it while they've got a room's attention.

**The ethos (same as everything):** artist keeps the money. No big-tech cut. Local-first. Optional, free.

## THE FLOW
1. **Artist books a slot in advance** (openmic) → we know who's playing, where, and *when*.
2. **Booking confirmation offers merch:** "You're booked at [venue] on [date] — want merch there? Set up your merch page." (2-min, optional.)
3. **Artist sets up a merch page** (part of their openmic profile): shirts, stickers, CDs/vinyl, digital (tracks), zines, art. Each item = price + a buy/contact link that goes **straight to the artist** (their Stripe/PayPal/Bandcamp/insta — their money).
4. **A QR goes to the venue + the artist.** At the show it sits on the merch table / their set. Audience scans → buys.
5. **Physical stock they don't have?** Because it's booked ahead, there's a lead-time window:
   - Bring their own stock (default), OR
   - **Local print-on-demand** (partner a Melbourne print shop — local-first, not a big POD platform):
     order tees/stickers on the booking, printed + delivered to the venue/artist before the date.
   - Digital merch (tracks, art) = instant, zero inventory.

## WHAT WE BUILD (staged)
- **v1 (now):** artist merch page template (QR-buyable at the show) — the tangible piece. [BUILT]
- **v2:** hook into the openmic booking flow — confirmation email includes the "set up merch" prompt +
  auto-generates the artist's merch QR for that date.
- **v3:** local print-shop partner for on-demand physical merch with delivery-to-venue before the show.
- **v4:** venue merch board — the site's "what's on" shows tonight's artists + their merch QR.

## WHY IT MATTERS
- Emerging artists rarely sell merch at open mics (no setup, no lead time). This fixes both.
- Money stays with the artist. Venue looks good supporting local artists. openmic becomes the thing that
  doesn't just book you a slot — it helps you *earn* from it.
- Vs big tech: Bandcamp/Shopify/printful all take cuts + fees. This routes buyers **direct to the artist**.
