# Come build it with us 🤝

This can't be done by one person. That's not a disclaimer, it's the whole point. If you're reading
this, you're already the kind of person we need.

You don't need to be an expert. You don't need permission or a piece of paper. **Proof over education** —
if you can make one thing a little better, you belong here. We make mistakes, we own them out loud, and
we fix them together. That's how this works.

## You don't have to code to help

**Musicians & venues** — the most valuable thing you can do:
- Use the tools. Run a night. Then tell us what's clunky or broken (the [feedback page](https://donn-duinn.github.io/free-music-stack/feedback/) or open an issue).
- List your open mic or gig on the [calendar](https://donn-duinn.github.io/free-music-stack/calendar/).
- Connect us to a venue, an artist, or a mentor who'd get it.

**Everyone**
- [Sign the charter](https://donn-duinn.github.io/free-music-stack/petition/) and share it with one person who'd care.
- Spread the word. A share reaches more people than we ever could alone.
- Know a charity or support service? Send it for the [help page](https://donn-duinn.github.io/free-music-stack/help/).

**Writers, designers, translators** — docs, a prettier page, or making it work in another language all count.

**Coders** — see "Help wanted" below.

## For coders — it's simpler than you think

Every tool is a **single, self-contained HTML file**. No build step, no framework, no npm install.
Open it in a browser, edit, refresh. That's the whole loop.

1. Fork the repo.
2. Find a [`help wanted`](https://github.com/donn-duinn/free-music-stack/labels/help%20wanted) or
   [`good first issue`](https://github.com/donn-duinn/free-music-stack/labels/good%20first%20issue).
3. Make your change. Keep it small and reversible.
4. Open a pull request. Tell us what it does in plain English.

Don't worry about it being perfect. A rough PR that helps beats a perfect one that never gets sent.

## Run your own copy (any venue can self-host)

The whole site is static. To run your own:
- **Easiest:** fork this repo, then turn on GitHub Pages (Settings → Pages → deploy from `main`). Done, free.
- **Or:** download any tool's `index.html`, edit the `CONFIG`/`EVENTS` object at the top, and open it anywhere.
- The backends (tickets, petition) are Cloudflare Workers — see their `README.md` for the 3-minute deploy.

## Ground rules (short)

- Be kind. No gatekeeping. Everyone started as a beginner.
- Honesty over polish. If something's broken, say so.
- Every dollar goes to artists and venues. Nothing here extracts from them, ever.
- It stays free and open (MIT). That never changes.
