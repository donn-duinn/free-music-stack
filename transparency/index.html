💰 Where the money goes

Taken so far: $0. Nothing has been charged for anything, and it never will be to use the tools.

There's a hard cap on what the founder takes: just enough to live and keep building. Every dollar past that reinvests — hosting, the next free tool, keeping it free for artists and venues. No investors, no shareholders, no cut.

When funding turns on, it'll be fully visible on Open Collective — you'll watch where each dollar lands.
