First Nations languages
They aren't machine-translated here, and that's on purpose. These languages belong to their communities, and they should be shared by their people, on their terms, and paid for properly — not run through an AI that would get them wrong and has no right to. Every other translation on this page was done with AI, and we're honest about that. We won't do it to First Nations languages.

If you're a First Nations speaker and you'd want your language represented here, we'd be honoured. Reach out — daniel.j.hogben@gmail.com — and we'll do it your way, with respect and payment, not as decoration.
