The problem in one line: culture isn't scarce, it's gated — by money, location, language and status. So much beauty is hidden in the chase for money and status. The plan is to cut every fence.

The plan, in four moves

Proof-first. Each move is either done, running, or next — no vapour.
DONE ✔

1 · Build it and give it away

A full toolkit that gives grassroots music everything big tech charges rent for — ticketing, sites, direct sales, merch, open-mic sign-up, a what's-on calendar — and route 100% to artists and venues.

Status: live in 48 hours, free forever, open source. You can click every piece today.

NOW ▶

2 · Get it into hands, everywhere

Reach venues, councils, music bodies, radio and artists across the country. Land the first real venue as proof. Translate it so borders don't gate it.

Status: 18 orgs contacted, replies landing (a council sourcing a room, a state body sharing venues, a venue signed on). 13 languages live, heading to 200.

NEXT →

3 · Fund the work, and build it on proper foundations

Grants, patrons and sponsors — not to sell the tools, but to keep them free and hire people to build. A hard cap on what the founder takes; everything past it reinvests. Fully transparent.

Done right means done properly. Because this handles ticketing and sales money for other people's venues and artists, the first hires aren't developers — they're lawyers and accountants to set it up right, then a finance person to run the money. One self-taught builder should not be winging other people's money. It gets done by professionals, with open books.

Status: grant conversation booked, patrons approached, rails ready. It's a movement, not one person.

THE GOAL →

4 · Hand it to everyone, in every town, every language

Self-hosted by any venue, in 200 languages, run by a crew that grows one contributor at a time. The fences come down for good, and it no longer needs any one person to survive.

Status: the crew road is open — anyone can join and build. Proof over education, no gatekeepers.
