The story

Daniel (donn), 28, got priced out of the one thing that kept him going: live music, the pub, the open mic. So on a Tuesday night he started building the opposite of what everyone said AI would do to artists. Forty-eight hours later, it was live and given away for free.

He's not the most skilled person for the job, and he'll be the first to say so. He noticed a gap, stood up, and asked for help. Read the whole why, in his words →
